# ADR-NNNN — Title naming the one decision

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

**Status:** Proposed

## Context

The problem in a few lines: what forces a decision now. Facts, no judgment.

## Decision

What was decided, stated directly, with a bold lead sentence per rule.

## Consequences

A few bullets: what becomes easier, and what becomes harder.

## Alternatives Considered

Only options that were really weighed. Leave this section out when there were none.

### 1. Option name

**Why rejected:** one-line reason.

## Related Pages

Links to tickets, articles or other decisions. Leave this section out when there are none.
