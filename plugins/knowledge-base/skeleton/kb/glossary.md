# Glossary

A glossary gives the team one shared vocabulary. When business, product and engineering use the same word for the same thing, requirements get clearer and misunderstandings get rarer.

## Governance

- **Anyone may propose** a new term or a correction, as a pull request.
- **The person who decides for the team**, named in the [README](README.md#decisions), approves and merges it.

## Terms

Each term is a `###` heading followed by one or two sentences. Terms are grouped under `##` headings by topic and sorted alphabetically within a group.

## Decisions

### Architecture Decision Record (ADR)

A record of one significant technical decision: what was chosen, why, and which alternatives were rejected. See [About ADRs](adr/about.md).

<!-- section:pdr -->
### Product Decision Record (PDR)

A record of one significant product decision, written like an ADR from the product side. See [About PDRs](pdr/about.md).
